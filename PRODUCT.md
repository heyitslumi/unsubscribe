# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are players looking for a fast, satirical arcade browser game — people who recognize predatory SaaS cancellation flows, cookie walls, and dark patterns and want a punchy 90-second puzzle/reflex run they can beat and share via an itemized score receipt.

## Product Purpose

UNSUBSCRIBE is a dark-pattern boss rush played inside a simulated 3:14 AM desktop OS (`UNSUBSCRIBE OS`). The player has 90 seconds to cancel 5 sketchy SaaS free trials (`$212.48/mo` total) before auto-renewal strikes. Success means terminating all 5 subscriptions with `$0.00` in dark-pattern trap damage (`S` / `S+` rank) and generating a shareable thermal card statement receipt.

## Positioning

Unlike static dark-pattern galleries or plain form mockups, UNSUBSCRIBE turns real-world hostile UX into arcade boss mechanics inside a diegetic desktop OS shell — pairing manipulative corporate interfaces inside the browser viewport with an external meta-game telemetry sidecar (virtual debit card damage vs. monthly savings, live transaction log, trauma-based screen shake, hit-stop, and a 3 AM sticky note hint system).

## Operating Context

Played in short desktop or mobile browser sessions with instant restart and zero sign-up friction. Each stage is a self-contained component (`{ onClear, onPenalty }`) representing a specific SaaS dark-pattern archetype, ending in a copyable plain-text receipt designed for pasting into Discord or group chats.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current v1 campaign: 5 playable stages (`CloudNote+`, `ConsentWall™`, `FitPulse AI`, `VerifyHuman`, `OmniSaaS Corp`) with a 90.0-second global renewal countdown, procedural Web Audio synth SFX, trauma shake, hit-stop, particle bursts, and receipt grading (`S+` down to `F`).
- Built with Svelte 5, TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS v4 (`@lucide/svelte` icons); deployed as a static SPA to `https://dreamy-garden-e7gg.here.now/` and tracked in `https://github.com/heyitslumi/unsubscribe`.
- Planned expansion direction: grow into a replayable arcade with daily seeds, endless or custom levels, and optional leaderboard support while keeping the core stage contract modular.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: `UNSUBSCRIBE` (shell title: `UNSUBSCRIBE OS`).
- Voice: deadpan corporate satire, hyper-enthusiastic SaaS retention copy contrasted against frantic 3:14 AM financial panic.
- Characters & motifs: Nimbus the weeping CloudNote+ mascot, Chad from Customer Success (ConsentWall™ retention bot), Pulsey the crying kettlebell (FitPulse AI), and the VP of Retention final boss.

## Product Principles

1. **The interface is the enemy, not the player's controls:** dark patterns inside the fake browser should be loud, manipulative, and tricky, while the outer `UNSUBSCRIBE OS` HUD and controls remain crisp, legible, and fair.
2. **Every misclick and clear must feel physical:** pair every trap charge and stage termination with immediate audio, visual, and ledger feedback so the stakes read like an arcade game rather than a static web form.
3. **Keep stages modular for arcade replayability:** isolate stage mechanics behind a clean contract so new bosses, daily seeds, and endless rotations can slot in without rewriting the shell.
4. **Reward cleverness and accessibility:** preserve intentional skill shortcuts (such as keyboard `Tab + Enter` bypassing cursor-repelling buttons) as celebrated style bonuses.
